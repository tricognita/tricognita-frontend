# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Tricognita welcomes contributions from everyone. To keep this a productive community:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree about code, not people. Critique the idea, not the contributor.
- **Be patient.** Maintainers are often a small team; replies may take a few business days.
- **Assume good faith.** Most misunderstandings are misunderstandings, not malice.
- **Be specific.** "This doesn't work" is harder to help with than "I expected X, got Y, here's how I reproduced it."
- **Stay on topic.** Issue threads should be about the issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sharing private information (yours or anyone else's) without consent.
- Deliberately ignoring maintainer guidance after repeated correction.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact `conduct@tricognita.com`. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project, calibrated to the severity and pattern of the behavior.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication identified as part of the project.
